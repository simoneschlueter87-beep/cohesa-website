Our Technology

Automated Bonding Solutions

From micro-electronic encapsulation to structural automotive bonding — our automated bonding platforms handle every viscosity, every geometry, every production speed.

ProSeal

Automated hole covering system that replaces traditional plastic plugs with adhesive patches — applied robotically with over 99% line availability.

  • Up to 80% weight reduction per vehicle
  • 100M+ holes sealed annually
  • Circular cartridge refill system

NarrowSeal

Endless adhesive tape applied from spool for ultra-narrow display frame bonding — enabling curve radii as small as 2 mm with zero die-cut waste.

  • Widths from 2 mm on 2,000 m spools
  • No curing time required
  • Clean reworkability

BoxSeal

Acrylic foam tape for sealing EV battery pack enclosures — IPX7-rated waterproof protection with temporary reopening capability for inspection.

  • Gap fill from 1.7 to 4.1 mm
  • No cure time, instant seal
  • PFAS/PFOS-free formulation
