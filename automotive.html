EV Battery Thermal Management

Our automated bonding systems deliver exact bead placement of thermal interface materials (TIM), ensuring optimal heat dissipation for high-voltage battery packs. Precision application reduces air gaps, maximizing performance and longevity of battery cells.

Gap-filler and thermal paste dispensing
Real-time volumetric flow monitoring

Structural sealing of complete battery enclosures demands the highest levels of precision and consistency. Our 6-axis robotic bonding systems apply FIPG (Formed-In-Place-Gasket) sealants along complex 3D contours, guaranteeing watertight, flame-retardant protection for the entire battery assembly.
